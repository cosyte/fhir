WHAT THIS REPOSITORY CARRIES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------
This repository does NOT redistribute the corpus documents. `corpus/corpus.json`
declares each document by its path within the corpus, its byte count and its
SHA-256 digest; `scripts/corpus-fetch.mjs` retrieves the bytes at run time from
the commit above and refuses any document whose digest does not match. The
materialised documents land in `corpus/documents/`, which is git-ignored, so no
third-party document content enters this repository's history.

That layout is deliberate and it is a safety decision as much as a licensing
one. Committed content is not undone by a revert, and this repository's PHI
scanner treats what it walks as self-authored synthetic fixtures. Keeping the
documents out of the tree keeps both of those true without widening a single
allow-list entry.

The attribution above is carried here because the digests and paths in
`corpus/corpus.json` are derived from the corpus, and because a reader who runs
`pnpm corpus:fetch` does obtain Apache-2.0 licensed material through this
repository's own tooling.

The r4 half of this corpus is described by its own maintainer as "not
maintained" (its README distinguishes "r5: r5 test cases (actively maintained)"
from "r4: r4 test cases (not maintained)"). Breadth here is evidence about
agreement on real documents, not a claim that the corpus is current.
